## Tax-deductibility wording (#8) — APPLIED; status now confirmed
Original site wording: *"Every contribution to our 501(c)(3) is tax-deductible."* — an unqualified, universal promise.
**Now live:** *"As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, donations may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law."*

**Owner confirmed the 501(c)(3) status on 2026-10-25.** The wording is nevertheless **kept as-is, deliberately**: confirmed 501(c)(3) status makes the *organization* eligible to receive deductible contributions, but it does not make every contribution deductible for every donor (itemizing, AGI limits, quid-pro-quo benefits received, and donor residency all affect this). Reverting to "is tax-deductible" would restore the overpromise.

Optional refinement if the owner wants it: append *"Please consult your tax advisor."*
