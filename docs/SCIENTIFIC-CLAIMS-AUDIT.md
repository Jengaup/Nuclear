## Cross-cutting guidance
- Prefer **NAP's own study, DOE, NRC, INL, national labs** as primary evidence. Do **not** rest a contested technical claim on a single promotional/explainer page.
- Distinguish **goal vs. fact** ("can lower prices" = projected, not guaranteed).
- Do **not** imply NRC/DOE **endorsement**; "applied NRC criteria" ≠ "NRC-approved." (See TERMS draft and PARTNERS audit.)
- If any claim cannot be sourced, propose precise wording and **wait for approval** — do not silently alter the number.
