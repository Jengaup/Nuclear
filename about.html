About · Nonprofit · Est. 2015

Engineers from the island, for the island

The Nuclear Alternative Project is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit created by Puerto Rican engineers working across the U.S. nuclear industry — united to study and educate about advanced nuclear energy as a clean, resilient path for Puerto Rico.

§ 01Why we exist

Mission & vision

Mission

Inform, so Puerto Rico can decide

To inform the people of Puerto Rico about the innovations and capabilities of advanced nuclear reactors — so the island can make an informed decision about the best way to meet its energy goals.

Vision

A greener, stronger tomorrow

A greener, more technologically advanced Puerto Rico, powered by a clean energy portfolio that includes nuclear — reliable enough to withstand the next storm.

§ 02Our story

How it began

In late 2015, Eddie Guerra — a seismic engineer — picked up the phone and called his college friends: Angel Reyes, a PWR senior reactor operator, and Jesús Nuñez, a structural engineer. All three were Puerto Rican engineers working in the U.S. nuclear industry, and all three saw the same thing.

Before advanced reactors could ever transform Puerto Rico's energy sector, people first needed to understand their benefits and their challenges. When Jesabel Rivera, a community-engagement expert, joined the team, the mission truly came to life — turning rigorous engineering into clear conversations with the communities who would live with these decisions.

"Together, we can help Puerto Rico enter a greener, more technologically advanced tomorrow."
